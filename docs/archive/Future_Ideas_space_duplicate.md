# FabricVision-AI — Future Enhancements & Roadmap (V2+)

This document captures features deferred from V1 to keep core synthesis and virtual try-on pipelines deterministic, lightweight, and robust.

---

## 1. Fit Customization & Measurements

Fit customization is explicitly separated from V1 core generation. The following capabilities are planned for V2:

- **Fit Categories**:
  - Slim Fit
  - Regular Fit
  - Loose / Oversized Fit
  - Relaxed Athletic Fit
- **Custom Measurement Mapping**:
  - Chest, Waist, Hip, Inseam, and Shoulder width numerical input parameters.
- **Body Shape Adaptation & Tailoring**:
  - Dynamic 3D garment mesh warping based on body shape parameters (Hourglass, Pear, Rectangle, Inverted Triangle).
  - Virtual tailoring adjustments (tapered waist, hem adjustments, darting).

---

## 2. Advanced Multi-Garment Layering & Outfits

- Simultaneous multi-garment virtual try-on (e.g. layering a jacket over a shirt and pairing with trousers).
- Fabric texture transfer across custom outfit combinations.
